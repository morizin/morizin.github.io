Mohammed Rizin — Machine Learning Engineer

I'm a machine learning engineer. Right now I'm on contract with Shipd / DataCurve, and earlier this year with Mercor — work I can name but not describe. What I can talk about is on this site: the projects, the competition record, and the writing.

Before that, four years of production engineering. Founding ML researcher at Medulla, where I built an LLM-backed clinical assistant with a retrieval-and-citation pipeline over a curated medical corpus and led a team of three through three pivots. Deep learning engineer at Fathom Solutions, doubling a road-damage detector's mAP and shipping it to the browser with TensorFlow.js. Deep learning intern at NVIDIA, where I learned distributed multi-GPU training, Docker and Triton-served inference, and wrote conversion scripts that shipped as Deep Learning Institute course material.

I'm a Kaggle Competitions Master — 12 medals, most of them in code competitions where the organiser runs your submission against a hidden test set under a fixed compute budget — and I'm finishing two degrees at once: a B.Tech in Computer Science at Amity University Mumbai and the online BS in Data Science at IIT Madras, both due in 2028. I'm from Malappuram, Kerala, and work from Mumbai.

Projects

  • Movement Safety — Can synchronised accelerometer and gyroscope data from consenting phones characterise a physical interaction? A Rust research pipeline bootstrapped on two synthetic generators — one hand-authored, one MuJoCo — with the recall collapse between them published as the finding.
  • MNIST in hand-written CUDA — A two-layer MLP trained on MNIST where every kernel — tiled matmul, fused bias+ReLU, fused softmax cross-entropy, batch gather, SGD-momentum — is written by hand. No cuBLAS, no cuDNN.
  • Crash Detector — Predict an imminent crash from dashcam frames, vehicle sensors and current actuator state — then intervene to stabilise the vehicle in the seconds before impact.
  • Upstage: Making AI Beneficial — A 0.966 solution to Upstage's sequential-learning competition: a SAINT transformer, pseudo-labelled retraining, and an ensemble across seven training variants — all code and weights public.
  • Tuberculosis Detection — Binary TB screening from chest X-rays with a Keras model behind a one-page Streamlit app — upload a radiograph, get a call. Research and education only.
  • Jigsaw: Agile Community Rules — Silver medal — 82nd of 2,445 — predicting whether a Reddit comment breaks a subreddit's rule: a config-driven pipeline, Qwen3-8B fine-tuned with a from-scratch GRPO implementation against a model-graded reward, and a cross-validated ensemble with BGE and DeBERTa-v3.
  • AI Village CTF @ DEFCON 31 — A solo silver — 36th of 1,344 — across the AI Village capture-the-flag challenge set: attacks against deployed models and the guardrails wrapped around them.
  • Google Research Football with Manchester City — 348th of 1,139 in a multi-agent self-play environment where the opponent pool exploits your agent's weaknesses instead of scoring it against a fixed test set — and where hand-authored controllers kept beating learned policies.

Services

  • Evaluation & task design: Coding tasks, verifiers and benchmark sets for training or evaluating agents — the golden solution, the specification a model must satisfy, and the deterministic check that decides whether it did, packaged to run in Docker.
  • CUDA & GPU performance: Reference kernels, profiling and multi-GPU training speed-ups — from a slow training loop to a fused, measured implementation.
  • Computer vision, prototype to production: Detection and classification systems on real data, deployed where they actually have to run — server, browser or edge device.
  • RL & simulation research: PPO and GRPO from scratch, model-graded rewards, MuJoCo or Gymnasium environments — and the evaluation that says whether the policy learned the task or the reward.
